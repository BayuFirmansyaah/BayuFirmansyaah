A large secondary monitor behind it has a minimal technical system graph, softly out of focus. A compact mechanical keyboard, a small architectural desk model of connected modular buildings, dark desk surface. Left 40% is almost-black negative space for large HTML headings. Composition wide 16:9; laptop and monitor occupy right 60%, elegant dramatic perspective, realistic materials, black studio background #0B0D0C. Subtle acid lime #D7FF35 screen glow and soft silver edge lighting, tactile keycaps, sophisticated editorial mood, quiet focus and technical precision. Beautiful high-end product photography, not gamer aesthetic. No helmet, no racing car, no person, no brand logo, no watermark, no text overlays, no neon city, no fake statistics.

Glass blocks, precise metal connectors, miniature lecture halls and stacked academic records represented abstractly as frosted glass layers. Isometric three-quarter high-angle view, strong geometric clarity and visual hierarchy, premium architecture model photography, not a cartoon. Very dark forest-green background #131A14, acid-lime #D7FF35 accent, warm ivory white architecture. Wide 3:2 composition centered on the model with clear padding, cinematic softbox light and realistic shadows. Elegant quiet technology art direction. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no people, no actual product UI, no fake metrics.
